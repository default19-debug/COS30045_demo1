How to use this

Comparing honestly

The one rule that makes this data useful: compare within a screen size, not across. Filter to the size you have already decided on, sort by kWh/yr, and the cheapest television to run is at the top. That comparison is valid. Sorting the whole register by kWh and buying the top result just tells you to buy a small television.

The official consumer version of this tool is the government's own Energy Rating product comparator, which searches the same registration database. It is the authoritative place to check a specific model before you buy.

A caveat worth keeping: the kWh/year figure is a laboratory measurement taken under one standard test, with a fixed picture setting and a fixed 10 hours a day. Your own consumption depends on brightness, picture mode and what you actually watch. The figure is excellent for comparing models and only approximate for predicting your bill.
