Data & method

Where these numbers come from

Everything on this site is derived from one public file. This page records where it came from, what was wrong with it, every change made to it, and the things it cannot be used to claim.

1. Source and governance

Australia and New Zealand run the Greenhouse and Energy Minimum Standards (GEMS) scheme. A television cannot legally be sold in either country unless the supplier registers it, submits energy test results measured under a prescribed standard, and carries the Energy Rating Label. The registration database is published openly.

This matters for how the data can be read: it is not a survey or a sample. It is an administrative census of the legal market. There is no sampling error to worry about, and no response bias. What there is instead is registration bias, described in the limitations below.

Privacy assessment

The dataset contains no personal information. Every record describes a manufactured product, and the only named parties are companies acting as registrants, which is commercial rather than personal data and is published deliberately as part of a regulatory disclosure regime. No anonymisation, aggregation or suppression was required before publishing these visualisations, and no re-identification risk arises from joining it with anything else on this site.

Derived attributes

inches

screensize / 2.54, rounded. Converts the published centimetre diagonal into the unit the product is actually sold in.

kwh_per_m²

Energy per square metre of panel. Lets brands be compared without rewarding those that mostly sell small sets.

cost_aud_year

kWh × tariff. The only figure on this site that uses an assumption rather than a measurement, so it is exposed as a slider.

How the site’s files are produced. knime/pipeline_reference.py repeats every KNIME step in Python and adds the final shaping the charts need: tidy names for the aggregate columns, the spread and cost columns for Act four, the star × band pivot for the heat map, the 25-model cut for brands, and key_facts.csv. Its output in data/out/ is what the site loads. KNIME’s own writer output is kept, unedited, in data/knime_out/ and checked against it by knime/verify_knime_outputs.py: all 4,599 models, 8 bands, 830 65-inch models and 22 brands match value for value, with three explained exceptions. cost_aud_year is $1 lower for 43 models, because KNIME multiplies by the floating-point 0.35 where the script computes ×35÷100; 106 unknown tuner values are left as text rather than recoded to missing; and one JVC model registered with the model number NA keeps that text in KNIME but is read as missing by pandas. None of the three reaches a chart.

4. What this data cannot tell you

Being honest about the edges of a dataset is part of presenting it. Five limits apply to every chart on this site.

It is a lab figure, not your bill

The kWh/year value is measured under one prescribed test with a fixed picture setting and 10 hours of use a day. It is excellent for comparing models on equal terms and only approximate for predicting a household's actual consumption.

Registered is not the same as stocked

A registration means a model may legally be sold, not that any retailer carries it. Some registrations are years old, which is why a 2017 commercial panel can appear alongside current models. Availability is self-reported by the supplier.

No sales weighting

Every model counts once, whether it sold a million units or none. A median across models is not a median across televisions in Australian homes, and this data cannot be used to estimate national consumption.

Supplier-reported test results

Figures are submitted by the registrant under the scheme's test standard, with regulatory check-testing rather than independent testing of every model. Believability rests on the compliance regime.

The tariff is an assumption

35 c/kWh is a national-average figure; real rates vary by state, retailer and time of use. No conclusion on this site depends on it — the energy ranking is identical at any price, and the slider lets you substitute your own.

Standby is excluded

Standby power is in the file but missing for 854 rows, so it is left out of every chart and headline figure rather than imputed.
